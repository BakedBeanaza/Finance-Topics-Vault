# Post Lectio
Revisit FINMAN
- **Capital Budgeting**
	- The process of planning expenditures on assets with cash flows that are expected to extend beyond 1 year.
- **Capital Structure**
	- the specific mix of debt, preferred stock, and common equity that a firm uses to finance its operations and purchase assets.
- **Dividend Policy**
	- the decision by a firm’s management regarding how much of its earnings should be distributed to stockholders as dividends versus how much should be retained and reinvested in the firm.
- **Working Capital Management**
	- daily process of balancing a company's short-term assets and liabilities—specifically inventory, accounts receivable, and accounts payable—to ensure smooth operations and strong cash flow
$$Working\ Capital=Current\ Assets-Current\ liabilities$$
